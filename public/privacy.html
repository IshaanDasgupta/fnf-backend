Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Friendly Floors ("Friendly Floors", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Friendly Floors mobile application and related services ("Service").

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Friendly Floors, we may collect the following information.

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Age
  • Gender
  • Phone number when you use phone-based authentication
  • Google account identifier when you use Google Sign-In
  • Profile and flatmate preferences

Property and Listing Information

If you create or interact with listings, we may collect information such as:

  • Property details
  • Rent and other costs
  • Location and locality
  • Property images
  • Furnishing and occupancy information
  • Amenities
  • House rules
  • Availability information
  • Other information you voluntarily provide in a listing

Location Information

With your permission, Friendly Floors may access your device's location while you use the app.

We use location information to help show listings near you and provide location-based search functionality.

You can disable location permissions through your device settings. Some location-based features may not work correctly if permission is disabled.

Usage and Technical Information

We may automatically receive certain technical information when you use the Service, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Request and error information
  • Basic service usage information

2. How We Use Information

We may use information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide and personalize listings and search results.
  • Show listings near your location.
  • Enable interactions between users.
  • Process and display property listings.
  • Maintain and improve the Service.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Protect the security of the Service.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Authentication Services

Friendly Floors may provide authentication through third-party providers such as Google and phone verification providers.

When you use Google Sign-In, we may receive information provided by Google that is necessary to authenticate your account.

When you use phone authentication, your phone number may be processed by our verification provider to deliver and verify authentication codes.

4. Location Data

Location access is used to provide location-based functionality, such as finding listings near you.

Friendly Floors does not require continuous background location access for the core location feature described above.

5. How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Friendly Floors, including providers for:

  • Cloud hosting
  • Database infrastructure
  • Authentication
  • Phone verification
  • Maps and location services
  • Security and technical operations

These providers process information on our behalf or as necessary to provide their services.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Friendly Floors, our users, or others.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Information Visible to Other Users

Some information you choose to provide may be visible to other Friendly Floors users.

For example, information contained in a property listing or profile may be displayed to users of the Service.

Do not include sensitive personal information in public profiles or listings.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to:

  • Access information associated with your account.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account or personal information.
  • Withdraw certain permissions.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the information below.

10. Children's Privacy

Friendly Floors is intended for people who are at least 18 years old.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 through the Service.

11. Third-Party Services

Friendly Floors may use third-party services to provide functionality such as authentication, maps, hosting, database services, and phone verification.

Those services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

12. Account Deletion

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.
